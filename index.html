Questions

Why don't you list prices?

Every part is quoted for the specific truck and delivery location, so you get one firm number instead of a list price plus surprises.

Can I return a part?

Unpainted parts can be returned within a short window after delivery, subject to a restocking fee. Painted parts cannot be returned — always dry-fit before paint.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Parts carry a one-year manufacturer warranty against defects.

Do you charge sales tax?

Fleets installing on their own trucks pay Texas sales tax. Shops buying to resell can provide a Texas resale certificate (Form 01-339) and keep it on file with us.

Do you carry lights and mirrors?

Coming soon. Lighting and mirrors are federally regulated safety parts, and we will list them once the compliance documentation is in hand. Ask and we will let you know when they are available.

Where do you deliver?

We operate out of the Houston area and serve shops and fleets across Texas. Call and we'll work out delivery for your location.
